# Abstract scaffold (研究目的 / 方法 / 结果 / 结论)

Per 白皮书 ch.3: one standalone paragraph, concrete with numbers, no literary flourish, no
citations/figures/complex formulas. The student writes the bracketed pieces; the skill critiques.

---

**[研究目的 / Purpose — 1–2 sentences]**
What question do you answer and why does it matter? (The research gap — your words.)

**[研究方法 / Method — 1–2 sentences]**
How did you do it? (Math: what you prove and the technique. Experimental: design + key
measurement. CS: method + datasets + baselines. Econ: identification strategy.)

**[研究结果 / Results — 2–3 sentences, WITH NUMBERS]**
What did you find? Specific, quantified. Avoid "improvement was observed" — give the magnitude.

**[研究结论 / Conclusion — 1–2 sentences; the LAST sentence is your contribution, in YOUR words]**
What does it mean, and what is the one contribution that is yours?
