# What actually wins, by subject

Distilled from the LOCAL winners archive (`winners/*/README.md`, 393 entries 2020–2025) and
白皮书 ch.2 (各学科热门选题). These are **patterns for learning, not templates to copy** —
imitating a prior winner's exact method in the same subject is penalized (SKILL.md guardrail §4).
Every named paper below is a real entry in the archive; do not invent others. When you need a
fresh, topic-specific list, run `scripts/mine_winners.py "<keywords>"`.

## 数学 Mathematics
- **Texture of winners:** self-contained problems in number theory, combinatorics, geometry,
  group theory, analysis. The student poses a sharp question and proves something.
  Real examples: "On Chen's Theorem, Goldbach's Conjecture and Applications of Sieve Methods"
  (2024 铜), "Isoperimetric and Isodiametric Problems with Constraints in Euclidean Space"
  (2024 铜), "A Study of the Carathéodory Conjecture through Non-Rotationally Symmetric Surfaces"
  (2020 金), "Investigation on i-commuting probabilities on finite groups" (2020 银).
- **What wins:** a clean theorem with a complete, student-owned proof. Modeling-flavored
  math also places ("Optimizing Dart Throwing Strategies … via Markov Decision Process",
  2024 金) when the model is genuinely novel.
- **What loses:** AI-assisted "proofs", surveys with no new result, problems too large to close.
- **Panel temperament (ch.4):** pure mathematicians (丘成桐, 李骏, 肖杰, 朱熹平 lineage). Expect
  whiteboard re-derivation. Deep AI suspicion.

## 物理 Physics
- **Texture:** strong tradition of *table-top experimental* physics on everyday phenomena, plus
  astro/condensed-matter/acoustics. Real examples: "The Inseparable 'Paper Vice' — Friction
  Amplification in Interleaved Assemblies" (2024 银), "Investigation of Rotational Dynamics in
  Asymmetric Acoustic Fields … Acoustic Levitation" (2024 铜), "The Dance of Light Under
  Ultrasound: … Sonoluminescence" (2024 优胜), "An Investigation into the Sound Amplification
  in a Music Box" (2024 优胜).
- **What wins:** a curious physical phenomenon, carefully measured, modeled, and explained.
  "Hardware + AI" hybrids place strongly (e.g. a self-built spectrograph + AI classifier).
- **What loses:** "let GPT list some equations and I plugged in numbers" — judges probe boundary
  conditions, dimensional analysis, the Hamiltonian.

## 化学 Chemistry
- **Texture:** materials, electrocatalysis, sensors, biodegradable materials, computational
  screening. Real examples: "Improving Intracellular Synthesis Efficiency of GFP Catenane
  through Directed Evolution" (2024 金), "Inkjet Printing of Photonic Crystals for Photothermal
  Responsive Structural Color" (2024 银), "Computational Screening and Design of MOFs for CO2
  Separation" (2024 优胜).
- **What wins:** computational chem (DFT, MOF/COF design, ML scoring) is welcomed; experimental
  synthesis wants real lab work with AI limited to literature/characterization.

## 生物 Biology
- **Texture:** neuroscience, gene editing, microplastics/environment, AI-guided drug/vaccine
  design, multimodal health sensing. Real examples: "Exploring the 'Brain Switch' of Alcoholics
  … Liraglutide" (2024 银), "Development of a Gap-Driven Base Editing System" (2024 优胜),
  "AI-Guided Design and Preliminary Validation of Anti-Tuberculosis Subunit Vaccine" (2024 优胜).
- **What wins:** the "AI screen + real wet-lab validation" closed loop (ch.7 案例五). Judges
  drill data provenance, ethics approval, batch effects, independent validation sets.

## 计算机 Computer Science
- **Texture:** LLMs, generative models, multimodal, agents, applied vision/health. Real examples:
  "LLM Mathematical Reasoning Grounded with Formal Verification" (2024 金 — LLM + Lean
  verification, a paradigm project), "Diagnosing Autism Spectrum Disorder via
  Brain-Population Graph-in-Graph Neural Networks" (2024 银), "Decoding the Past: Oracle Bone
  Character Recognition via ViT + GAN restoration" (2024 铜).
- **What wins:** an independent contribution *beyond* calling an API — a new training paradigm,
  a new benchmark, a new explanation of model behavior, or domain-knowledge fused with a model.
  Using AI is the entry ticket, not the edge.

## 经济金融建模 Economic & Financial Modeling
- **Texture:** carbon/environmental econ, game theory, market microstructure, applied
  econometrics, AI-as-object-of-study. Real examples: "Importing for Producing: The Net Effect
  of Carbon Regulation on Emission Reduction" (2024 金), "Design of Optimal Government Carbon
  Offsetting Mechanism" (2024 银), "The Role of a Credit System in Breaking the Iterated
  Prisoner's Dilemma" (2024 铜).
- **What wins:** a clean **causal-identification** strategy (DID/IV/RDD/experiment) with stated
  assumptions and robustness checks. The highest-frequency deduction (ch.7) is reading
  correlation as causation.

## Cross-cutting winning signals
- Schools recurring at the top (Beijing Normal Experimental, Renmin University HS, Nanjing
  Foreign Language, Shenzhen Middle, Shanghai HS Int'l Div, …) — context only; do NOT tell a
  student their school determines their odds.
- Teams (1–3) are common and welcomed (ch.1 合作精神: 2021 physics — 8 of 10 awards were teams).
- The recurring through-line across all six subjects: **one original idea the student can
  defend in English, on a whiteboard, under expert questioning.**
