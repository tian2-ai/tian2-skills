# Yau judging criteria & competition structure

Distilled from 白皮书 ch.1 (竞赛介绍 / 备赛重点 / 评审流程 / 奖项设置). Use this to ground
feasibility and significance scoring, and to set student expectations honestly.

## The six subjects + the cross-discipline Grand Prize

数学 (Mathematics) · 物理 (Physics) · 化学 (Chemistry) · 生物 (Biology) ·
计算机 (Computer Science) · 经济金融建模 (Economic & Financial Modeling).

Plus **科学金奖 (Grand Prize)** — one cross-discipline award per year, drawn from the strongest
finalist across all subjects. (e.g., 2024 Grand Prize: the shark-skin-riblet antithrombotic
heart-valve paper, also Biology Gold.)

## Award structure per subject (ch.1 §奖项设置)

Each subject: **金奖 1 · 银奖 1 · 铜奖 3 · 优胜奖 5**. Prizes: 金 15万 / 银 10万 / 铜 6万 /
优胜 3万 RMB. Plus the single cross-discipline **科学金奖 (15万)**.

**2025 change (18th edition):** a new **入围奖 (Finalist Award)** tier was added below 优胜,
for teams that reached the national finals but did not medal. This lifted total awarded teams
from ~60 to 87. Reaching the finals is now itself a recognized honor.

Actual counts each year are set by the international panel based on submission quality and
defense performance — they are not guaranteed.

## The 备赛重点 — four phases, four emphases (ch.1)

1. **课题选取 (topic selection).** "一个课题的优劣程度，直接决定了该参赛作品能走得多远."
   Confirm with your advisor along three axes:
   - **可行性 (feasibility):** Is the theory within a HS student's reach? Is the experiment
     actually doable (equipment, venue, time)? Can it finish before the deadline?
   - **创新性 (originality):** 理论创新 (extend/deepen a theory to a novel conclusion) or
     应用创新 (apply an existing theory in a new scenario — usually needs experimental
     validation, else "纸上谈兵").
   - **社会意义 (social significance):** Many winning works connect to a real-world problem.
     A plus, not a requirement.
2. **科研流程.** If you decide to enter in June, plan ~2–3 hours/day until the paper deadline,
   balanced against coursework, standardized tests, other competitions.
3. **论文写作.** Must satisfy: 学术诚信 (data integrity, independence) · 逻辑与完整性 ·
   专业性 (correct format, complete structure, proper conventions). Weighs heavily in the
   初赛 (preliminary) round.
4. **演讲答辩.** After reaching the semifinal, build a presentation and rehearse repeatedly,
   anticipating judge questions. (See `/yau-defense-coach`.)

## The competition pathway (ch.1 §评审流程) — mainland track

报名 (Apr 15 – Jul 31) → 提交/更新论文 (Aug 1 – Sep 15) → **分赛区评审** (初筛/函评/桌评/
分赛区决赛, Sep 16 – Nov 6) → 论文公示 (Nov 7–13) → **全国总决赛** (English defense at Tsinghua,
mid-Dec) → 科学论坛. Overseas (Asia / North America) tracks have their own calendars — see
`/yau-pathway-navigator`.

## How feasibility/significance map to the rubric

- **Feasibility (F):** score down if the theory needs grad-level machinery the student can't
  acquire in time, or if the experiment needs inaccessible equipment. Score up for clean,
  bounded scope finishable by the Sep deadline.
- **Significance (S):** score up for a genuine question (applied OR pure). Do not require
  社会意义 — a beautiful pure-math result scores on its own merits. Penalize "应用创新" claims
  with no validation ("纸上谈兵").
