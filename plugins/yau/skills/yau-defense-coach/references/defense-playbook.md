# Defense playbook — format & per-subject panel temperament

From 白皮书 ch.1 (评审流程) + ch.4 (评委背景) + ch.7 (Q&A patterns).

## The format (ch.1)
- 2026 全球总决赛 at Tsinghua, December 5–6 (subject to final notice; page-rule.html verified 2026-09-09). Students AND guiding teachers must attend the interview/defense. **All-English oral defense**, run by the international
  review committee. (Asia/North-America tracks defend earlier in their own rounds.)
- The semifinal (半决赛) also adds a defense round before the finals.
- Judges question the paper in depth; they assume AI use and probe understanding.

## Per-subject panel temperament (mirror of judge-backgrounds)
- **数学:** pure mathematicians. **Expect to re-derive your key lemma on the whiteboard.** Deep AI
  suspicion. Master every proof step.
- **物理:** condensed-matter/statistical/computational physicists. Expect boundary conditions,
  dimensional analysis, the Hamiltonian. "Hardware+AI" welcomed; pure-AI rarely wins.
- **化学:** synthetic/materials/nano chemists. Computational chem welcomed; for synthesis, defend
  real lab work + characterization.
- **生物:** cell biology/virology/genetics. They drill data provenance, ethics approval, batch
  effects, independent validation. "AI screen + wet-lab validation" is the respected loop.
- **计算机:** graphics/vision/HPC/systems. They ask training DETAILS (data split, attention dims,
  baseline fairness), not concepts. Edge = a contribution beyond the API.
- **经济金融建模:** macro-finance/econometrics/game-theory. The decisive question is causal
  identification (instrument, exclusion, parallel trends). Correlation-as-causation = top deduction.

## Universal panel posture (ch.7)
- They ask details, not definitions.
- They want "why this problem / why this approach / why it matters."
- They want the one original idea AI couldn't supply.
- The death answer is "我用了 GPT-4o 跑了一下."
