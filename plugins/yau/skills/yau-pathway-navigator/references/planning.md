# Planning a Yau run

Plan backward from the paper-submission deadline. Dates below follow the official 2026 cycle
(source: https://www.yau-awards.com/page-rule.html — verified 2026-08-27); re-confirm the
current year on the official site.

## Single-year plan (mainland, 2026 calendar)
Anchor = 报名+提交 window **Jul 1 – Sep 15** (registration and paper submission run in the
same window; paper stays updatable until Sep 15). Work backward:
- **By Apr–May:** pick subject (`/yau-subject-navigator`) and topic (`/yau-topic-finder`); line up
  a mentor if needed (`/yau-mentor-finder`).
- **Jun–Aug (the research window):** ~2–3 hrs/day once committed (ch.1). Do the research; draft as
  you go (`/yau-research-plan-drafter` → `/yau-paper-writer`); analyze (`/yau-data-analysis-tutor`);
  handle AI disclosure + chat-log retention (`/yau-ai-compliance`). Register early in the
  Jul 1 – Sep 15 window rather than waiting.
- **By Sep 15:** submit the paper (deadline for both registration and submission).
- **Sep 16 – 30:** 官方材料审核 — make sure the submission package survives it (报名信息、
  PDF 封页/目录/致谢页、诚信声明、查重报告 ≤10%、AI 使用说明与聊天记录).
- **Oct 1 – Nov 2:** 分赛区评审; if you advance, prep the defense (`/yau-defense-coach`).
  公示 Nov 3 – 10.
- **Dec 5 – 6:** 总决赛 English defense at Tsinghua.

Overseas tracks: Asia is earlier — registration due **Jun 11**, full report due **Aug 17**
(https://yauaward-asia.hk — verified 2026-08-27); North America runs synchronized with the
mainland Jul 1 – Sep 15 window (http://www.yau-science-awards.org/signup — verified
2026-08-27). Same skills, shifted calendar.

## Reality check (ch.1 / ch.6 framing)
- Balance against coursework, standardized tests, and other competitions — don't let the Yau run
  capsize the overall plan. The award helps applications but ranks below GPA/standardized scores.
- Reaching the finals (入围奖, introduced 2025 — confirm current cycle) is itself a recognized
  honor — a strong, defensible paper that reaches finals beats an over-ambitious one that never
  finishes.

## Multi-year build (for a younger student)
- **Year 1 (e.g. grade 9–10):** build foundations — coursework depth in the target subject, read
  papers (ch.3 reading method), maybe a small practice project. Learn 自己出题自己做.
- **Year 2:** a real entry with a focused, self-owned topic; aim to reach finals.
- **Year 3:** a stronger, more original entry building on accumulated skill.
Avoid the trap of an over-scoped first attempt; depth and ownership compound across years.

## Common timeline mistakes
- Starting topic selection in August (too late to do real research before the Sep 15 deadline).
- Letting mentor-hunting (4–8 weeks) delay the research itself — start both in parallel.
- Ignoring the Sep 16–30 材料审核: an incomplete package (missing 查重报告, AI 说明, 诚信声明)
  can sink a good paper before any judge reads it.
- Treating the defense as an afterthought — it decides medals; rehearse early.
