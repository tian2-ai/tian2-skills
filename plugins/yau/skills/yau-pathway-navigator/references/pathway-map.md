# The Yau pathway — tracks, calendars, divisions, awards

From 白皮书 ch.1 (赛区划分 / 评审流程 / 奖项设置). **Dates are representative; confirm the current
year on the official site.** All three tracks converge at the 总决赛 at Tsinghua (all-English
defense, mid-December).

## Three tracks (eligibility by location)
- **中国内地** — mainland HS students (高中 only). Register: www.yau-awards.com
- **海外·亚洲** — HK/Macau/Taiwan + other Asian countries. Register: https://yauaward-asia.hk
- **海外·北美** — other overseas countries. Register: http://www.yau-science-awards.org/
Teams: 1–3 members, from the same school.

## 中国内地 calendar (representative)
- **Apr 15 – Jul 31:** 网上注册报名 (register; deadline Jul 31 24:00).
- **Aug 1 – Sep 15:** 提交/更新论文 (submit/update the paper; deadline Sep 15 24:00).
- **Sep 16 – Nov 6:** 分赛区评审 — four stages: 初筛 → 函评 → 桌评 → 分赛区决赛. Each region
  selects finalists for the national 总决赛.
- **Nov 7 – 13:** 论文公示 (finalist papers posted publicly online).
- **Dec 10 – 11:** 全国总决赛暨颁奖典礼 — **English oral defense, international panel, Tsinghua.**
- **Dec 11:** 科学论坛 (science forum / general lectures).

## 海外·亚洲 calendar (representative)
- **March:** registration opens.
- **Jun 30:** registration + research outline (研究大纲) due.
- **Jun–Jul:** outline preliminary review → finalists announced (by email).
- **Aug 31:** full research report due.
- **September:** report review/evaluation → results.
- **Late Oct:** defense + results (subjects staggered), award ceremony.
- **Late Nov:** science lecture. (Top teams advance to the Tsinghua 总决赛.)

## 海外·北美 calendar (representative)
- **May 18 – Jun 30:** register (deadline US-Eastern Jun 30 24:00).
- **Jul 1 – Sep 5:** submit/update paper.
- **Sep 6 – Oct 18:** paper review.
- **Oct 25 – Nov 20:** regional subject defenses.
- **Nov 20:** US regional award ceremony.
- **Dec 10 – 11:** 总决赛 at Tsinghua (finalists), English defense + forum.

## Awards (ch.1 §奖项设置)
Per subject: **金奖 1 · 银奖 1 · 铜奖 3 · 优胜奖 5.** Prizes: 金 15万 / 银 10万 / 铜 6万 /
优胜 3万 RMB + trophy & certificate. Plus one cross-discipline **科学金奖 (15万).**
**2025 (18th edition) new 入围奖 (Finalist Award):** below 优胜, for teams that reached the
national finals without medaling — lifted total awarded teams from ~60 to 87. Reaching finals is
now itself a recognized honor. Actual counts each year are set by the international panel based on
quality + defense performance.
