# The Yau pathway — tracks, calendars, divisions, awards

Structure from 白皮书 ch.1; **all dates below updated to the official 2026 cycle**
(verified 2026-08-27 against the official sites cited inline). Deadlines can still shift —
re-confirm on the official site for your track before relying on any date. All three tracks
converge at the 总决赛 at Tsinghua (all-English defense, early December: **Dec 5–6, 2026**).

## Three tracks (eligibility by location)
- **中国内地** — mainland HS students (高中 only). Register: www.yau-awards.com
- **海外·亚洲** — HK/Macau/Taiwan + other Asian countries. Register: https://yauaward-asia.hk
- **海外·北美** — other overseas countries. Register: http://www.yau-science-awards.org/signup
Teams: 1–3 HS students from the same school, with 1–2 指导老师. Training/for-profit
institutions are **prohibited** from coaching entries.
(Source: https://www.yau-awards.com/page-rule.html — verified 2026-08-27)

## 中国内地 calendar (2026 official)
(Source: https://www.yau-awards.com/page-rule.html — verified 2026-08-27)
- **Jul 1 – Sep 15:** 报名 + 提交论文（合并窗口；报名与提交/更新论文同期进行，
  deadline Sep 15）。Note: the pre-2026 two-stage schedule (spring registration, then a
  separate summer submission window) no longer applies.
- **Sep 16 – Sep 30:** 材料审核（官方对提交材料的合规审核：报名信息、论文 PDF、
  诚信声明、查重报告、AI 使用说明等）。
- **Oct 1 – Nov 2:** 分赛区评审。
- **Nov 3 – Nov 10:** 公示（finalist papers posted publicly online）。
- **Dec 5 – 6:** 全国总决赛暨颁奖典礼 — **English oral defense, international panel,
  Tsinghua.** 分赛区面试 30 分钟仅学生到场；总决赛 30 分钟师生到场（导师发言 1–2 分钟，
  学生英文 PPT + 问答）。(Source: https://www.yau-awards.com/page-assessment.html —
  verified 2026-08-27)

## 海外·北美 calendar (2026 official)
(Source: http://www.yau-science-awards.org/signup — verified 2026-08-27)
- **Synchronized with the mainland 2026 schedule**: registration + paper submission through
  the North America portal on the same Jul 1 – Sep 15 rhythm, followed by review, regional
  evaluation, and advancement of finalists to the Tsinghua 总决赛 (**Dec 5–6**, English
  defense). Confirm exact regional-round dates on the NA site.

## 海外·亚洲 calendar (2026 official)
(Source: https://yauaward-asia.hk — verified 2026-08-27)
- **February:** registration opens.
- **Jun 11:** registration deadline (报名截止).
- **Aug 17:** full research report due (报告截止).
- **Oct – Nov:** defenses in Hong Kong (subjects staggered) + regional results.
- **December:** finalists advance to the Beijing/Tsinghua 总决赛.

## Regional divisions (mainland, 2026)
2026 structure: **数学奖设北/南/东三赛区；其余五科（物理/化学/生物/计算机/经济金融建模）
设北/南两赛区** — this replaced the older 华北/华东/华中/华南/西部 five-region system.
(Source: https://www.yau-awards.com/page-rule.html — verified 2026-08-27)

**数学 (3 regions):**
- 北部: 北京/河北/天津/山西/内蒙古/新疆/黑龙江/吉林/辽宁/湖北/河南/陕西/宁夏/甘肃/青海
- 南部: 广东/广西/海南/四川/湖南/贵州/云南/西藏/重庆/福建/江西/安徽/澳门
- 东部: 浙江/江苏/山东/上海
**物理/化学/生物/计算机/经济金融建模 (2 regions):**
- 北部: 北京/河北/天津/山西/山东/内蒙古/新疆/黑龙江/吉林/辽宁/河南/陕西/宁夏/甘肃/青海
- 南部: 浙江/江苏/上海/广东/广西/海南/四川/湖南/湖北/贵州/云南/西藏/重庆/福建/江西/安徽
Overseas tracks are organized by region office, not these provincial splits.

## Awards (2026 official)
Per subject: **金奖 1 (¥5万) · 银奖 1 (¥3万) · 铜奖 3 (¥1万) · 优胜奖 5 (¥0.5万)** +
trophy & certificate, plus one cross-discipline **科学金奖 (¥5万)**.
(Source: https://www.yau-awards.com/page-rule.html — verified 2026-08-27)

Historical note (白皮书, 2025 cycle): the 18th edition added an 入围奖 (Finalist Award)
below 优胜 for teams that reached the national finals without medaling, lifting total awarded
teams from ~60 to 87 — reaching finals became a recognized honor in itself. Confirm on the
official site whether the current cycle retains it. Actual counts each year are set by the
international panel based on quality + defense performance.
