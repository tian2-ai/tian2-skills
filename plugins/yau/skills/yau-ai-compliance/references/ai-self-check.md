# Pre-submission AI-use self-check (13 items)

> 2026-09-09复核：先读 `official-rules-2026-09-09.md`；导师事先许可、人工核验与数据保护是前置条件。披露必须包括名称及版本、环节及用途、时间及频率；相关AI聊天记录必须上传“其他材料”。下方教学建议不是官方追加门槛。


## Mandatory official checks before the teaching checklist

- [ ] Prior teacher permission is evidenced, not retrospectively invented.
- [ ] Student owns selection/design/core arguments/verification/academic expression.
- [ ] Literature, citations, formula/code/results, copyright and data security manually checked.
- [ ] No secret, sensitive or unauthorized data uploaded to AI.
- [ ] Complete tool/version, stage/purpose, dates/times/frequency disclosure.
- [ ] Related chat logs actually uploaded to “其他材料”; gaps declared.

## Teaching checklist (mark non-applicable model/baseline items N/A)

Items 1–12 in the spirit of 白皮书 ch.7 §自查清单; item 8 and item 13 reflect the official
2026 rules (https://www.yau-awards.com/show-86-59.html, verified 2026-09-09). Any unchecked
item = a remediation task before you
submit. Walk the student through each; mark ✓ / ✗.

1. [ ] I can explain every AI-assisted passage and restate it in my own words without AI.
2. [ ] I can derive every key formula / algorithm step on a whiteboard, unaided.
3. [ ] I can present the whole study (question, method, results, conclusion) orally, no slides.
4. [ ] I can state my training data's source, size, cleaning rules, label process, and biases.
5. [ ] I can state the model's architecture, parameter scale, hyperparameters (lr, batch size,
       epochs), and training hardware.
6. [ ] I ran at least one full cross-validation or independent-test evaluation, results match the
       paper, and I kept the logs (W&B link or local CSV).
7. [ ] I compared against ≥3 baselines (naive / strong classic / recent SOTA) and can justify
       the choice.
8. [ ] I disclosed every AI use on the Acknowledgements page with all complete official fields —
       工具名称及版本 / 具体环节及用途 / 使用时间及频率 — plus Methods detail where AI is a substantive
       method component (official 2026 rules).
9. [ ] I cited the original papers for any pretrained model / dataset / algorithm I used, and did
       NOT paste AI-generated citations.
10. [ ] I prepared answer frameworks for "what role did AI play" and "could you do it without AI."
11. [ ] I locally reproduced every AI-generated figure/number and saved the executable script.
12. [ ] The abstract's key sentences, the intro's research-gap sentence, and the conclusion's
        contribution sentence are MY OWN — not AI-rewritten or restructured.
13. [ ] I retained and exported my COMPLETE chat logs with every AI tool I used, organized by
        research stage, matching the Acknowledgements disclosure one-to-one, and actually uploaded under Other Materials for
        review (pending uploads remain unchecked) (聊天记录备查 — official 2026 rules; no deletion, cherry-picking, or
        after-the-fact reconstruction).

Closing (ch.7 结语): AI's role at the Yau award is shifting from "explain why you used it" toward
"explain why you didn't." But the standard never changed — did you pose a question worth asking,
do you understand your methods, and is there one original idea that is truly yours? The tools are
new; the standard is old.
