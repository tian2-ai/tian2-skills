# AI-use disclosure templates

> 2026-09-09复核：先读 `official-rules-2026-09-09.md`；导师事先许可、人工核验与数据保护是前置条件。披露必须包括名称及版本、环节及用途、时间及频率；相关AI聊天记录必须上传“其他材料”。下方教学建议不是官方追加门槛。


From 白皮书 ch.7 §披露与诚信. Place in the paper. Adapt to what the student ACTUALLY did — never
misrepresent. Disclosure is REQUIRED by the official 2026 rules
(https://www.yau-awards.com/show-86-59.html, verified 2026-09-09): the Acknowledgements page
must state each use's 工具名称及版本 / 具体环节及用途 / 使用时间及频率, and the matching chat logs must be
retained for submission. These templates organize the complete required fields.

## Student-completed disclosure worksheet (not submission-ready prose)

For EVERY tool, have the student fill real facts, not copied examples:
- Tool name / provider / exact version: [student fills]
- Research stage and specific purpose: [student fills]
- Actual dates/time period and frequency: [student fills; unknown stays unknown]
- Teacher permission obtained before use: [who / when / retained evidence]
- Inputs: [authorized material only; no sensitive/secret/unapproved data]
- Accepted/rejected output and manual checks: [citations/formulas/code/results/copyright]
- Student's own decisions, experiments and writing: [specific contribution, not blanket claim]
- Related chat-log files and upload location: [files uploaded to Other Materials / 其他材料]
- Missing records or unresolved prohibited use: [truthful gaps; never fabricate]

For substantive AI methods, the student additionally supplies real model/dataset citations,
licenses, hyperparameters, hardware, split/evaluation design, and reproducibility evidence.
Do not invent sample counts, GPUs or results. Review the student's own acknowledgement;
do not write a polished blanket declaration asserting independent work without evidence.
For the mainland overall Acknowledgements page, also check mentor relationship/payment,
all participants' stage-by-stage contributions, difficulties and resolution, and the
1–2-page Word / 500–1500-character requirement in the official baseline.

## Answering "did you use AI?" at defense (layered disclosure)
Framework (ch.7 §答辩如何回应):
> "Yes, for {X} I used {tool} to {specific task}, mainly because {reason}. However, {core insight
> / decision} was mine, reached through {specific reasoning}; AI was not decisive there. I can
> derive/demonstrate that part now if you'd like."

Avoid both extremes: panicked denial (detectable, fatal) and over-confession (makes it sound like
AI did most of the work). Steer the conversation to the detail you own best.

## What NOT to do (ch.7 §常见误区)
- Don't call trivial AI polishing "no AI use" — small lies, once caught, sink overall credibility.
- Don't paste AI-generated citations.
- Don't put AI-derived statistics in the paper without local reproduction (keep an executable
  notebook).
