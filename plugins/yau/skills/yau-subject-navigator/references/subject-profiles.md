# The six Yau subjects — fit profiles

> 2026-09-09规则优先：先核 `official-rules-2026-09-09.md` 的适用赛区、许可/数据安全、主体本人完成、伦理/机构签章、完整披露和提交要求。下方为学科教学/历史经验，并非新增官方硬门槛；数字评分、训练时长、基线数量、排期建议不代表官方规则。历史获奖医疗/药物题目不是本季许可，纯理论生物研究并不因没有湿实验自动不合格。


Distilled from 白皮书 ch.2 (热门选题) + ch.4 (评委背景) + the local winners archive. Real judges
and real winner titles only. Use to give honest fit verdicts.

## 数学 Mathematics
- **You should be:** someone who enjoys *proving* things and can pose your own problem (奥数 hands
  you a problem; 丘奖 asks you to find one). Comfort with rigor and abstraction.
- **Access needed:** pencil + paper + literature. No lab, no GPU required.
- **Panel:** pure mathematicians (朱毅, 许洪伟, 叶俊, 吴康; advisory 丘成桐/李骏/肖杰/朱熹平).
- **What wins:** a clean theorem with a complete, self-owned proof; or a genuinely novel model.
  Texture: Carathéodory Conjecture surfaces (2020 金), sieve methods / Goldbach (2024 铜),
  isoperimetric problems with constraints (2024 铜), Markov-decision dart optimization (2024 金).
- **AI norm:** deep suspicion; AI only for checking/formal-verification/lit-search. **Whiteboard
  re-derivation expected at defense.**

## 物理 Physics
- **You should be:** curious about physical phenomena, willing to measure carefully and model.
  Strong table-top experimental tradition; also theory/astro/condensed-matter.
- **Access:** a bench and basic instruments help a lot; some theory/numerical projects need only
  a computer.
- **Panel:** condensed-matter/statistical/computational physicists (徐少达, 林熙, 邢向军, 吴镝,
  郑波, 阮东, 陈焱).
- **What wins:** an everyday phenomenon measured + modeled + explained ("Paper Vice" friction
  2024 银; acoustic levitation 2024 铜; sonoluminescence, music-box acoustics 2024 优胜).
  "Hardware + AI" hybrids place strongly.
- **AI norm:** fine in data analysis; cautious in theory modeling.

## 化学 Chemistry
- **You should be:** into materials, synthesis, catalysis, sensors, or computational chemistry.
- **Access:** wet lab for synthesis/materials; computational chem needs only compute.
- **Panel:** synthetic/materials/nano chemists (唐本忠 AIE, 黄乃正, 唐智勇, 李艳梅, 席振峰, …).
- **What wins:** GFP catenane directed evolution (2024 金), photonic-crystal inkjet printing
  (2024 银), computational MOF screening for CO2 (2024 优胜). Computational welcomes AI;
  experimental keeps AI to literature + characterization.

## 生物 Biology
- **You should be:** into neuroscience, genetics, environment, drug/vaccine design, or health AI.
- **Access:** wet lab or a mentor's lab for experimental arms; some computational-bio is
  laptop-feasible but the panel prizes the "compute + wet-lab validation" loop.
- **Panel:** cell biology / cell death / virology / genetics (袁钧瑛, 蔡亮, 俞强, 李文辉, 周敬流,
  郗乔然, 杨继).
- **What wins:** liraglutide & alcohol-addiction neural mechanism (2024 银), gap-driven base
  editing (2024 优胜), AI-guided TB vaccine design (2024 优胜). Panel drills data provenance,
  ethics approval, batch effects, validation sets.

## 计算机 Computer Science
- **You should be:** a builder who can program (PyTorch/HuggingFace) and wants to do something
  *beyond* calling an API.
- **Access:** a laptop + Colab can suffice for many projects; large training needs more.
- **Panel:** graphics/vision/HPC/systems (查红彬, 傅红波, 胡事民 Jittor, 过敏意, 童若锋, 张松海;
  advisory 姚期智).
- **What wins:** LLM + formal verification (2024 金), brain-graph ASD diagnosis (2024 银), oracle
  bone recognition via ViT+GAN (2024 铜). Using AI is the entry ticket; the edge is an
  independent contribution (new paradigm/benchmark/explanation).
- **AI norm:** "AI 就是这个学科本身." Panel asks training details, not concepts.

## 经济金融建模 Economic & Financial Modeling
- **You should be:** into economics/finance, comfortable with math modeling and data, and
  understand that **causal identification** is the heart of the subject.
- **Access:** data + a laptop. No lab.
- **Panel:** macro-finance/econometrics/game-theory economists (唐本忠 [finance], 苗彬, 王城,
  苏良军, 翁翕, 汤珂, 张顺明, 白重恩, 周亚虹).
- **What wins:** carbon-regulation net effect (2024 金), optimal carbon-offset mechanism design
  (2024 银), credit systems in the iterated prisoner's dilemma (2024 铜). AI as research-object
  is a strong pattern.
- **AI norm:** ML/NLP/RL for data & prediction is fine; **never** let an LLM "reason" the
  economic mechanism — judges ask for your instrument, exclusion restriction, parallel trends.

## 科学金奖 (Grand Prize) — cross-discipline
One award per year, drawn from the strongest finalist across all six subjects (e.g. 2024: the
shark-skin-riblet antithrombotic heart-valve work, also Biology Gold). You do not "enter" it
directly — you enter a subject and may be elevated. Cross-disciplinary breadth (the award's
"开阔眼界" value) is what tends to reach this tier.
