# Research-plan critique rubric

Apply after the outline is drafted. Output must-fix vs nice-to-have. Grounded in 白皮书 ch.1
(学术诚信/逻辑与完整性/专业性) and ch.3.

## 1. Originality (the doctrine)
- Can you point to the one idea that is yours? If the whole plan is "known method on known data,"
  flag must-fix: add the student's own angle (the "一点儿原创性").
- Same-subject clone of a prior winner's method → flag.

## 2. Feasibility (备赛重点)
- Will this finish before the Sep paper deadline with the stated hours and resources?
- Does the theory/method exceed what the student can acquire and DEFEND in time?

## 3. Per-subject rigor
- Math: every proof step the student's and whiteboard-reproducible; prior results cited.
- Experimental: controls explicit; variables defined; sample size justified; ethics/data
  provenance addressed.
- CS: ≥3 baselines; ablations isolate the contribution; train/val split stated; mechanism
  (not "it learned it"); the independent contribution named.
- Econ: identification strategy stated with its key assumption; robustness checks present;
  causation vs correlation explicitly separated.

## 4. Logic & completeness (逻辑与完整性)
- Does the outline tell one coherent story from question → method → result → significance?
- Are there orphan sections or gaps marked `[student to provide]` that are actually core
  (must-fix) vs. peripheral (nice-to-have)?

## 5. Defense-readiness
- For each section, could the student defend it in English at a whiteboard under expert
  questioning? Flag any section that relies on something the student can't reproduce.

## Output format
```
MUST FIX (before you start writing / before submission)
- [section] issue → concrete action

NICE TO HAVE
- [section] suggestion

STRONGEST PART
- one honest compliment so the student knows what to protect
```
