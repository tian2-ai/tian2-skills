# Disclosures & disqualification triggers

The mandatory disclosures live in **Task 4** (Science Research Description) and **Task 5** (Research
Report Upload) of the Regeneron STS application, not in Task 3 itself — but they sit alongside the
Rules Wizard and an omission can cause **failure to qualify**. This skill surfaces and tracks them; it
does NOT write the student's narrative answers (route those to `/sts-contribution-coach` and
`/sts-mentor-finder`). Verbatim/derived from `Regeneron_STS_Application_Questions_2026.txt` and
`rubric.json`.

---

## §1 — The four failure-to-qualify disclosures

### Payment disclosure — Task 4 Q6 ("Research Payment Disclosures")
Select all options that apply regarding payments. STS requires disclosure of **any** payments made by
you or on your behalf for your research experience or training.
- **"Entrants who do not disclose involvement in a paid program will be disqualified."** (official)
- All disclosed payment types are **permitted and very common**.
- Credit **any** level of coaching — including application, essay, or competition coaching.
- If you have **ever paid a mentor/coach**, even for a different project or circumstance, disclose it.
- "I" includes anyone who made payments on your behalf.
- Option families in the dropdown include: paid to work on research (wage/stipend); paid a fee/hourly
  rate for research or mentorship; paid for help with paper/essays/application; scholarship to a
  fee-charging program; paid a fee to a research program; paid for housing/food/travel; paid only to
  be connected to a mentor; paid a coach for general advice; paid for training/classes then mentored
  free; paid a mentor/coach who also supported the STS entry free; "none of the above but there were
  fees I want to explain"; "none of the above, no fees."

**DQ trigger:** non-disclosure of involvement in a paid program.
**Routing:** payment context and how to frame it → `/sts-mentor-finder`.

### Conflict-of-interest disclosure — Task 4 Q18 / Q18a
"Required Conflict of Interest Disclosure — Do you need to report a real or perceived conflict of
interest?" You must acknowledge any employer / mentor / donor / family connection (real or perceived).
- Consider: related to anyone in the lab; related to anyone who knows your mentor; familial/long-term/
  pre-existing relationship to a mentor or recommender; parents knowing your mentors in any capacity;
  mentor being a grad student in a relative's lab/institution; pre-existing relationship to Society
  for Science staff or trustees.
- This is **permitted and not penalized** — but **"Non-disclosure could lead to an entry's failure to
  qualify for competition."** (official)
- **Q18a** (200 words max): describe the relationship(s), name the individuals, and describe their
  involvement.

**DQ/failure-to-qualify trigger:** non-disclosure of a real or perceived COI.
**Routing:** drafting the Q18a / independence narrative → `/sts-contribution-coach`; mentor-relationship
framing → `/sts-mentor-finder`.

### AI-use disclosure — Task 4 Q15 (and Q19)
**Q15:** "Did you use AI in this project or Research Report or application in any way? If so, describe
how AI was utilized and what programs were used." (100 words max)
- **Q19 (Research Report Similarities, 150 words max):** explain anything in the report that might be
  flagged by a plagiarism or AI detector (common protocols, AI use). Properly cited + mentors credited
  = acceptable.
- Note the tension with the **Ethics Statement (Task 10)**, which certifies the entrant did NOT use AI
  tools (e.g. ChatGPT) to **construct** the research report or application responses. Q15 disclosure is
  about how AI was used **in the project/analysis**; it does not license AI-ghostwritten report or
  application text. This skill structures and critiques the student's own words — it never ghost-writes.

**DQ-adjacent trigger:** AI-generated essays/reports flagged by integrity screening (`rubric.json`).
A high score does not guarantee surviving the AI/plagiarism/COI integrity screens.

### Statement of independence — Task 4 Q20
"Statement of Independence" (200 words max): reassure the evaluation committee that, while the
influence of parents/mentors/relatives/friends/other students may exist, the submitted work is your
own. Disclose anyone who closely or loosely guided you, shared methodology, or contributed — and their
relationship to you and your family.
- Official: "Failing to disclose similar or related research of which you are aware or failing to
  mention any person who has either closely or loosely guided you, and their relationship to you and
  your family, is a violation of our rules and the ethics statement."

**Failure-to-qualify trigger:** failing to disclose related research you are aware of, or a person who
guided you. STS is **individual** — independence and honest attribution are central.
**Routing:** drafting Q20 → `/sts-contribution-coach`.

## §2 — Citation rule (Task 5 Research Report Upload)

- **Every single image / chart / graph / table must be properly cited per the Citation Guide.**
- **Images/charts/tables/graphs that YOU created must also be cited** per the guide.
- **"Lack of citation or an incorrect citation could result in disqualification."** (official)
- Evaluators and judges are **not permitted to click links** — do not link to external websites except
  as part of your bibliography.
- A paper that only reviews literature or only outlines a forthcoming plan **without results** is not
  eligible.

**Routing:** report structure, page/file limits (≤20 pages, ≤4MB, filename `LASTNAME.FIRSTNAME.ZIPCODE`,
not a scanned-image PDF), and citation formatting → `/sts-research-report-coach`.

## §3 — Individual-project pledge (Task 4 Q24)

"Combining individual research projects into team projects to submit to other competitions is against
STS Rules. An individual project must remain so through competition season." Verify you have no plans
to combine the STS research with another high-school student for a different competition.
- "I DO NOT have plans to combine this research with another high school student for competition."
- "I DO have plans to combine … thus am NOT eligible for Regeneron STS."

Related: Q23 Intellectual Property — confirm with the scientists you worked with that they have no
concerns about sharing the IP for the competition; confirm you have permission to share.

## §4 — DQ / failure-to-qualify trigger summary (from rubric.json + Official Rules)

- Team projects involving **other high-school students** on the submitted work.
- AI-generated essays/reports (flagged in integrity screening).
- Research Report **> 20 pages**.
- Plagiarism / **uncited** content (incl. uncited self-made figures).
- **Missing required approvals** (IRB/IACUC/biosafety/etc.).
- **Non-disclosure of a paid program** (automatic DQ).
- **Non-disclosure of a real/perceived COI** (Q18) — can fail to qualify.
- Failing to disclose a guiding person or related research (Q20) — rules/ethics violation.
- Submitting a literature review or a results-free plan (ineligible).

A "9999" not-scored sentinel in the internal docket = ranked in but parked pending integrity screening
(plagiarism/iThenticate, COI, AI, team/secondary). A strong score does not bypass these screens — so
the disclosures above matter independently of merit.
