## Quick orientation notes
- **The application = 10 tasks + the research report.** Tasks 4 and 5 carry the scientific weight;
  Task 7 carries the communication/potential signal; Tasks 2a–c are the only pieces other people
  submit and therefore need the longest lead time.
- **Project Category** (Task 4) is one of 20 options and only sets reviewer expertise — it does
  NOT change which prizes you're eligible for.
- **Confidential / not scored:** Task 10. **Optional:** Task 9. Everything else is scored or feeds
  a scored criterion.
- **DQ landmines** live mostly in Tasks 3, 4, and 5: missing approvals, undisclosed AI use,
  report >20 pages, uncited images/charts, plagiarism, or a team project with other HS students.
