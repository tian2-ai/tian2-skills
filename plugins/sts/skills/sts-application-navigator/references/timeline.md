# STS backward-planning timeline

Anchor: the application **and all recommendations** are due **Thursday, November 6, 2025,
8:00 pm ET** for the 2026 cycle (customer-support deadline one day earlier; no exceptions).
ALWAYS re-confirm the exact date each cycle against the Official Rules — deadlines move yearly.

Everything below is planned **backward** from the deadline (T-0). The two scarcest resources are
(a) other people's time — recommenders and the counselor submit independently — and (b) drafting
+ revision time for the Research Report. Plan those first; they cannot be crammed at the end.

## Stage-based entry points
- **Scoping (no topic yet):** you are earlier than T-10 — start with `/sts-topic-finder` and
  `/sts-mentor-finder`, then re-enter this navigator once research is underway.
- **Building (research in progress):** prioritize the T-8 forms work and lining up
  recommenders/counselor now, even before the report is written.
- **Writing (research done):** start at T-6 (report draft) and run the report, contribution, and
  essay lanes in parallel; do not let recommendation lead time slip.
- **Final (almost done):** jump to the T-2 → T-0 self-score, compliance sweep, and submit-early
  steps.

## Non-negotiable reminders
- All recommendations are due at the SAME deadline as the application — they are not extra time.
- Approvals (IRB/IACUC/permits) and the counselor transcript are the most common things that run
  out of time. They are not under your direct control, so they go first.
- Submit early and download your full application. There are no deadline exceptions.
